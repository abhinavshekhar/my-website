# F Block copy voice

Warm, playful, hostel-proud. Short sentences. No corporate English.

## Poster lines

- Host line: `F BLOCK HOSTEL PRESENTS` (always caps)
- Slogan: 3–6 words, tradition vs tomorrow, home vs hostel, or play vs pride
- Surprise strip: `+ Some Special Surprises + WE'RE NOT TELLING YOU YET!`
- CTA: `— Wear Your {Theme} Best — SEE YOU THERE!`
  - Onam: Onam Best · Holi: Whitest White · Diwali: Ethnic Best
  - Sports: Jersey Best · Music: Night Best · Freshers: First-Impressions Best
- Credits: `ORGANIZED BY — {TITLE} CORE TEAM  F BLOCK HOSTEL`

## Instagram caption

Hook (1 line) → 3 activity bullets → logistics → 5–8 hashtags.

Hashtag bank (pick 5): `#VITChennai` `#FBlock` `#FBlockHostel` `#VITC`
`#HostelLife` `#Onam` `#Diwali` `#Holi` `#Pongal` `#Freshers` `#HostelDay`

Do not tag official VIT accounts unless the user asks.

## Tone checks

- Inclusive across states and languages. One vernacular line is enough.
- No roasting a wing, floor, or person on the poster.
- No “limited girls/boys only” copy unless the user is announcing a
  hostel-rule-compliant session and states it clearly.
